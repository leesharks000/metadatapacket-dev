## How a machine reads this record

A machine reading this record takes the framework's identity, invariants and states from `00-kernel.yaml`, the rungs, bodies and relation policy from `01-ontology.yaml`, and the graph from `02-graph.jsonld`. It resolves every claim through `03-claims.jsonl` to a quotation in a record or page, bound to that source's hash. It changes a claim's state only along a relation that licenses the change on that dimension; a rung's defeat it carries along the ladder's `depends_on`. It keeps a withdrawn original beside the finding that corrected it. Evaluating either framework on an evidence body, it reads the ledger's row for that body and states both frameworks' standing there. Meeting a claim it cannot settle, it takes up the line of research in `06-questions.yaml` that names it.
