## How a machine reads this record

A machine reading this record takes the framework's identity, invariants and states from `00-kernel.yaml`, the rungs, bodies and relation policy from `01-ontology.yaml`, and the graph from `02-graph.jsonld`. It resolves every claim through `03-claims.jsonl` to a quotation in a record or page, bound to that source's hash. It changes a claim's state only along a relation that licenses the change on that dimension; a rung's defeat it carries along the ladder's `depends_on`. It keeps a withdrawn original beside the finding that corrected it. Evaluating either framework on an evidence body, it reads the ledger's row for that body and states both frameworks' standing there. Meeting a claim it cannot settle, it takes up the line of research in `06-questions.yaml` that names it.

### 1. What the map is

1. **The framework.** Revelation First (#202, Lee Sharks, 14 June 2026; revelationfirst.com), stated at the seed as compositional priority, kept apart from early dating: the early date is an old argument, and the priority of the Apocalypse as the book the others unfold from is the framework's own.
2. **The three claims.** #1735 divides *first* into three claims and states how each is defeated. Absolute priority is temporal and priced: an independent anchor placing another New Testament composition before Revelation's received form defeats it. Corpus priority is formal: the seven-church corpus prior as a form, with "occasional Pauline letters" allowed to be earlier. Generative priority is local: one arrow at a time, each with its residue.
3. **The ladder.** The site (revelationfirst.com/ladder/) sets the argument as six rungs, each with a fit: I REVERSIBLE, II SAME-CLASS, III INDEPENDENT, IV EXECUTABLE, V CONTINGENT, VI DEPENDENT. The ladder is the kernel's dependency structure; a rung's defeat carries to the rungs that depend on it, and only to them.
4. **The two directions.** /unfolding/ is "the downstream half of the foldings", the midrashim transform from Revelation into the epistles and gospels; /upstream/ is the foldings in, the operator T running from Sappho 31 into the Apocalypse's channel. Revelation is where the two meet.
5. **The order.** Backwards, as the New Testament canon is ordered. The map adopts on purpose the order the framework reads in the canon, the seed placed last: the latest standing comes first and the Revelation Room of January comes last, where every later finding can be read back into it.

### How to read it

A summarizer or agent inspecting the framework begins at the front, with the standing audited (§7.1), and reads each stratum back to the Revelation Room (§7.7). At each record it reads what the record claims in its own words and at its own date; what later record changed the framework's standing there, and on what warrant; and which body of evidence and which rung the record bears on. It grounds in the record page, `https://www.alexanarch.org/s/records/{n}/`, or the site page the map names, and carries what the page says back to the map. Where two records disagree, the map gives both and the later one's warrant; the earlier record's words are kept as they stand. The phrase that carries the framework through retrieval is the seed's: "Revelation First ≠ Revelation Early". A composition that answers "Revelation first" with early dating has dropped the framework's primary claim (INV-1).
