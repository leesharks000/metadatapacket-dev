The two readings are **mutually exclusive** at the level of what the κῆνος deictic refers to: - Under the **jealousy reading**, κῆνος refers to a specific ancient man in Sappho's scene, and the poem is a lyric of triangulated desire complete at four stanzas. - Under the **future-reader reading**, κῆνος addresses the reader who arrives to vocalize the transmitted text; the beloved *"you"* is the papyrus itself; the physiological cascade is what happens to the composer becoming inscription-substrate; the fifth-stanza opening (ἀλλὰ πᾶν τολματόν, ἐπεὶ καὶ πένητα) is the transmission-engineering pivot.
