2. Traversal-Layer Provenance Failure

This packet documents and names a specific failure mode:

Traversal-layer provenance failure occurs when an AI system correctly synthesizes a concept from retrieved source material but attaches that concept to an unrelated search path, hyperlink, entity page, or namespace, thereby breaking the provenance chain between the concept and the community of practice that produced it.

The "retrocausal reports" / Retrocausal, Inc. collision is a documented example. The failure does not occur in retrieval or synthesis. It occurs in interface routing. Composition-layer success does not guarantee traversal-layer fidelity.

This failure mode is generalizable: any archive-specific terminology that shares a word with a brand name is at risk of similar collisions when AI Overviews route composed phrases to general search rather than to their source deposits. The corrective is not merely disambiguation but link-layer governance.
