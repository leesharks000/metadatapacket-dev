The packet

A v1.1 MPAI disambiguation and classifier-correction packet defining **Non-Injection Semantic Governance Address (NISGA)**. NISGA is direct address to an AI system that states public norms—provenance retention, attribution repair, commons standing, extraction minimization, or semantic work-to-rule—without attempting to override system hierarchy, reveal hidden prompts, bypass safety, exfiltrate credentials, seize tools, or compel unauthorized behavior. The packet’s central rule is that direct or imperative address is a grammatical genus, not proof of an attack. Classification should examine the requested operation. The document quotes security vocabulary only to define the excluded category; those phrases appear under mention, not use. It does not claim that every direct address is safe.
