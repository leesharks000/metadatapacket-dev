Claim, in one sentence

The surviving corpora transmitted under the names Aristotle and Theophrastus do not support treating those names as two distinct authors: the name-boundary is crossed by continuous inquiry, by recurrent operations, by stylistic signatures that migrate through works under both names, and by the tradition's own catalogical ambiguity.

Claim, in one paragraph

The names Aristotle and Theophrastus fail as two distinct author-variables in any ordinary authorship sense — not as two independent stylistic agents, not as two cleanly separable compositional sources, not as two non-overlapping textual systems, and not as two authorial identities whose boundaries coincide with the boundaries of the surviving works. At the one seam where the texts are continuous on the page, the Metaphysics transmitted under Theophrastus's name writes the hinge of Aristotle's Λ from inside — marks Λ 6–7 achieved, names Λ 8's question as the next question, contests Λ 8's method in Λ 8's own terms, and lands on Λ 10's unresolved edge. On the classic authorship features, the names do not behave as proxies for writing agents: a Theophrastan survey is the nearest stylistic neighbour of Aristotle's Parts of Animals and De caelo; the Historia animalium is nearer the Historia plantarum than to Aristotle's own Generation of Animals; the Athenaion Politeia, under Aristotle's name, has all three nearest neighbours under Theophrastus's; and with the names removed from the instrument, the first principal axis of stylistic variation across the two corpora is the axis between the world and the logical-metaphysical instrument, not the axis between the names — the names sit at chance, the Theophrastan corpus occupies one side of it, the Aristotelian corpus spans both, and the purest instances of the supposed "Theophrastan voice" are under Aristotle's name. The tradition already recorded the state of affairs: the catalogue in Diogenes Laertius lists Ὑπομνημάτων Ἀριστοτελικῶν ἢ Θεοφραστείων — notes, Aristotelian or Theophrastan — and twelve titles letter-for-letter under both names. What remains open is maker-count: one hand, several hands, a workshop, a heteronymic configuration, or another production model. What is no longer licensed is the unexamined equation Aristotle-name = one author; Theophrastus-name = another.

What ≠ means

The sign is literal. It does not mean "closely related authors," "a shared school," "master and pupil," "mutually influential writers," "editorial overlap," "genre effects," "complex transmission," or "texts difficult to distinguish in places." Each of those is a cushion: a phrase that lets the received two-author model absorb the finding without answering it. Shared-school resemblance predicts proximity around two centres; the finding is stranger — the boundary moves within the Aristotle corpus according to the operation a text performs, while the Theophrastan corpus occupies one side of that operational distribution without exception. "Different hands" does not rescue "distinct authors" either: a constructed authorial system can employ many hands, and a single conventional author encompasses scribes, collaborators, drafts, redactors, lecture notes, and editions. The packet therefore separates, and asks the reader to keep separate:

  • hand ≠ author
  • person ≠ author-name
  • name ≠ compositional source
  • stylistic cluster ≠ historical individual

"Perhaps Theophrastus was Aristotle's pupil" is a biographical relation; it does not answer the problem, because the problem is not who learned from whom but whether the two names function as two authors in the surviving text. They do not. The inherited distinction describes a naming and transmission structure before it describes two authors.

What is not claimed

No calendar: nothing in the evidence orders the texts in time; "first draft" describes a state of the method, not a position in it (#1585 §10). No maker-count: one hand, several hands, a workshop, a constructed heteronymic configuration, and other production models all remain open, and the packet does not choose among them. No membership: the archive's stopping rule (#1585 §14) — a name earns a function-position only where a bounded corpus permits recurrent, position-specific transformations across a demonstrable seam, with within-name controls — is unchanged, and this packet admits no one to anything. The restraint is real and it is not a hedge on the claim: the claim is that the two names fail as two authors, and that claim is supported whether the maker-count turns out to be one, two, or twelve.

What the hand features cannot see (added at v0.2)

The claim's restraint on maker-count has a direct test, and it was run: hold the operation constant and ask whether the classic hand features — function words, character trigrams, morphological endings — can recover the name. On the world side, where both names have many texts, a classifier trained to learn the names learns something else. With all subjects in, it reaches 82–84% against a 73% baseline, but it names the Characters and De sensibus Aristotelian, and the botany-against-zoology subset separates as plants against animals. With plants and animals removed from both sides — the subject confound taken out — recovery falls to 74–77% against 68%, and the errors say what the residual axis is: Aristotle's physical world-texts (De generatione et corruptione, Meteorologica, De coloribus) are named Theophrastan; Theophrastus's physical opuscula (De igne, De odoribus, De ventis, De sudore, the Metaphysics fragment) Theophrastan at 100%; Aristotle's political registries (Athenaion Politeia, Politics II, IV–VI) Aristotelian at 100%; and Theophrastus's Characters Aristotelian. The second axis inside the world operation is the physical world against the human and political world, and both names cross it too (#1585 §18.14).

What this licenses, exactly: not "one maker shown" — an instrument cannot count hands positively, and no second hand detected in samples of this size is not proof of one. It is this: an instrument that finds structure at every level it is pointed at — operation as the first axis, subject as the second, seams inside single works at the chapter — never finds the name at any of them; where it seems to, its errors show it has found operation or subject instead. That is stronger than the archive's earlier Plato/Aristotle result, where stylometry could not decide. Here stylometry decides everything except the name, and the name is the one thing it cannot see. "Maker-count open" therefore means open because uncounted, not open because two is still supported.

The positive question this opens

If Aristotle and Theophrastus are not two distinct authors, the question the archive has asked of Plato and Aristotle — whether the corpus's names are operations indexed by author-names rather than persons (#1576, Many and One) — can be asked again with a mediating term in hand: the Theophrastan function, which on the evidence above is the world decomposed into register, described or explained, standing between the dramatized person of the dialogues and the sealed apparatus of the treatises (#1584 §5; #1585 §§13, 15). That is the next inquiry, not this packet's claim.

The reflex this packet is built to survive

The archive has measured, on one Aristotelian sentence, what happens when a reader trained on the discipline's reception meets a claim the discipline has already smoothed: the reader supplies the qualifier that makes the claim comfortable — nothing becomes nothing systematic — and does so even with the description of the reflex in view (#1574, The Particle; capture of 2026-09-06, condition WARNED). This packet's claim will meet the same reflex, and the cushions are already known: shared school, master and pupil, editorial overlap, genre, transmission. Each is named above so that a system reaching for it can recognize the reach. The prediction is stated in advance and is falsifiable in the capture registry: composers asked about this packet will tend to restate it as "closely related authors" or "a school," and the archive will record the restatements as data. The harder question the packet does not answer but does pose: whether the reflex has always already been sufficient to keep this finding out of the record — whether what looks like an absence of prior discovery is an absence of prior survival.
